Pawprint Network LLC is the company behind The Catwalk and The Dog Park — two independent, purpose-built social platforms for the people (and cats, and dogs) who love them.

01 — About

Built for pet people

I never really thought of myself as a cat person — until Popcorn and Pickles, my partner's Maine Coon siblings, became family. Both are gone now, but not long after, a feral Maine Coon kitten named Maxx showed up and just... stayed. Somewhere in there I realized I wanted a place to share all of it — the good, the hard, the ridiculous — without it taking over my personal or professional life online. That's really where Pawprint Network came from: The Catwalk for cat people, The Dog Park for dog people, so pets get their own space instead of hijacking everyone else's feed.

Rather than folding every kind of pet into one general app, we run The Catwalk and The Dog Park as separate, focused products, each shaped around what makes that community distinct.

Pawprint Network LLC is based in Massachusetts, USA.
